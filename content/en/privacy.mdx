## What data do we process and why?

- **Contact form** — name, e-mail and the message. Purpose: replying to your enquiry and
  discussing possible cooperation (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — steps prior to a contract, and (f) —
  legitimate interest in handling the request). Retention: TODO(facts:privacy.retention).
- **Spam protection** — Cloudflare Turnstile verifies that you are human when the form is sent
  and processes technical browser data and your IP address for that purpose. The form cannot be
  sent without this check.
- **Traffic measurement** — Plausible, cookie-less and without identifying individuals.
- **Cookies** — the site stores no cookies; the language follows the page address.

## Who has access to the data?

- Vercel Inc. — website hosting (EU servers, Frankfurt).
- Resend — delivery of contact-form messages to our mailbox.
- Cloudflare, Inc. — Turnstile verification.
- Plausible Insights OÜ — traffic analytics (EU).

## What are your rights?

You have the right to access, rectify or erase your data, to restrict processing, to data
portability and to object. Complaints can be lodged with the Czech Office for Personal Data
Protection (uoou.gov.cz). Send requests to TODO(facts:organization.email).
