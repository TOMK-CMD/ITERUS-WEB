Innea Pro is an Iterus product: a practice-operations system for Czech psychotherapists,
psychologists and coaches — from a solo practice to a group one. Clients, a self-booking calendar,
session notes with an AI draft, invoicing under Czech rules, health-insurer billing documents,
supervision and a client portal. It runs on the same core as Innea and is live.

## What problem does Innea Pro solve?

A therapy practice is, to a large extent, administration: bookings and reminders, a note after every
session, invoices, insurer billing, supervision and continuing education — and in the Czech
Republic each of those comes with its own rules and formats. Innea Pro puts the whole practice into
one system that understands Czech billing and reporting, and connects it to the Innea client app.

## What did we build?

- **Calendar** — day, week, month; client self-booking, SMS reminders (BulkGate), a waiting list,
  recurring appointments, multiple providers and rooms, Google Calendar sync and Czech public
  holidays.
- **Session notes** in SOAP, DAP, BIRP or free format, plus validated scales (PHQ-9, GAD-7 and
  others) with trends. Session recording with every participant's consent → transcript → AI draft
  note → therapist approval; nothing is stored as a note without that approval.
- **Invoicing under Czech rules** — PDF invoices, QR payments (SPAYD), ISDOC/UBL export, credit
  notes, payment matching from Fio, the Comgate payment gateway, export to Fakturoid, iDoklad and
  Pohoda, VAT variants.
- **Health insurers** — billing documents and batches in the KDAVKA/FDAVKA formats.
- **Client work** — homework, therapy goals, a safety plan, case formulation. **Innea Connect**: a
  client from the Innea app can link to their therapist, who then sees an AI pre-session summary —
  only with the client's explicit consent.
- **Client portal** with passwordless sign-in (a link sent by e-mail), secure messaging, workflow
  automation, practice administration and supervision including continuing-education tracking.

## How did we work?

<HowWeWork />

The division of labour is the same as for Innea and is described in detail in
<PageLink href="/references/innea">its case study</PageLink>: the human — the project's
technical lead — made the decisions on architecture, security model and process, and Claude Code
implemented to specification with tests and review. Innea Pro added Czech billing and reporting on
top — the ISDOC, SPAYD and KDAVKA/FDAVKA formats, the VAT rules — where the human decided what
gets built and in what order, and verified the result in production just as for Innea.
