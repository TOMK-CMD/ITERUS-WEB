## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
  <FaqItem question="Why are there no client logos?">
    Because we have no client references — everything here is our own product, which we scoped,
    built and paid for ourselves. Instead of logos we show what other studios cannot because of
    NDAs: the architecture, the test and migration counts and the integrations used, straight from
    the repository in every case study.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="Can we see the code?">
    We don't publish the source code of our own products. We're happy to go through the
    architecture, test coverage and integrations on a call, though — and on your project the code
    is yours on payment, so the question doesn't arise there.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="Is the project finished, or still in progress?">
    The status is written on every project — live, pre-launch or in development — and a case study
    puts it in numbers: what is tested and deployed, not what is planned. For an unlaunched
    product we promise no launch date; we say what the launch is waiting for.
  </FaqItem>
</Faq>
