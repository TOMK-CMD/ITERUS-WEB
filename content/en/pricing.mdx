## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
  <FaqItem question="What does the smallest meaningful project cost?">
    A validation pilot — a working version in production with authentication, a database, one or
    two integrations, tests and deployment — runs 90,000-250,000 CZK and takes 3-6 weeks.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="Fixed price, or hourly?">
    We publish bands by scope, not a published hourly rate — for the pilot and the production
    version alike, you know the scope up front. We work out the exact number on a free
    introductory call.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="What happens to the price if I change the scope?">
    We work in short iterations, so a small change is folded into the next one. A bigger scope
    change affects the estimate for the remaining work, not what's already done and paid for.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="What's included in the price?">
    The pilot includes authentication, a database, one or two integrations, tests and deployment.
    The production version adds roles and permissions, payments, admin, more integrations, e2e
    tests, CI and monitoring — the exact scope is listed under each band above.
  </FaqItem>
</Faq>
