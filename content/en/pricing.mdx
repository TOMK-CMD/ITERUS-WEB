## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
  <FaqItem question="What does the smallest meaningful project cost?">
    A validation pilot at 90,000–250,000 CZK excluding VAT, done in 3–6 weeks. It is a working
    version in production — authentication, a database, one or two integrations, tests and
    deployment — not a demo prototype. If you don't yet know exactly what to build, a paid
    discovery for 25,000 CZK comes first, deducted from the project price.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="Fixed price, or hourly?">
    By scope band, not by hours worked: for the pilot and the production version alike you know
    the price and scope up front, and nobody counts hours. That is why we publish no hourly rate —
    what you buy is a finished thing in production, not time. The exact band is settled on the
    introductory call, which is 60 minutes and free.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="What happens to the price if I change the scope?">
    A smaller change is folded into the next step — we work in short iterations. A bigger change
    of scope affects only the estimate for the remaining work, not what is done and paid for; you
    hear about it immediately, not on the invoice.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="What's included in the price?">
    The pilot includes authentication, a database, one or two integrations, tests and deployment;
    the production version adds roles and permissions, payments, admin, more integrations, e2e
    tests, CI and monitoring. Handover is always included: on payment you own 100% of the source
    code and documentation. Running costs (hosting, model APIs) are not included; we work them out
    on the consultation.
  </FaqItem>
</Faq>
