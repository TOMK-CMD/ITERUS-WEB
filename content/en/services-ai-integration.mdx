We connect company data to the Claude or OpenAI API through a RAG or agentic workflow — with
answer-quality controls and a transparency layer under the EU AI Act. Data is hosted in the EU
(Frankfurt) and processed under GDPR; the third-party model is never trained on your data.

## What's included?

AI integration and on-prem LLM work starts from 180,000 CZK (on-premise deployment from 350,000
CZK because of hardware) and includes connecting the model to company data, answer-quality
controls and a transparency layer.

We already run an EU AI Act transparency layer in production in our own product, Innea — the user
learns they're talking to AI from the very first sentence, not partway through.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
  <FaqItem question="Does our data stay in the EU under GDPR?">
    Yes — we host in the EU (Frankfurt) and process data under GDPR, not under terms outside
    Europe.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="Is the model trained on our data?">
    No, as long as we use the API in business mode (not a free consumer account) — we check the
    specific provider's exact terms with you before starting.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="What if the AI gives a wrong answer — who's liable?">
    A human reviews the answer before it becomes a binding offer or decision — the AI gets a
    clearly scoped role, not unlimited decision-making.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="Do we have to tell users they're talking to AI?">
    Yes, for a chat or assistant — it's an EU AI Act (Article 50) requirement, and we build it in
    from the start rather than bolting it on later.
  </FaqItem>
</Faq>
