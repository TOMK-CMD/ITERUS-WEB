On-premise LLM deployment means queries and data stay on your own hardware — nothing leaves the
company network. We deploy open-source models on your own server or workstation; on-premise
deployment starts from 350,000 CZK (hardware not included).

## Who is this for?

Companies where sensitive data (health records, contracts, internal documents) must not leave the
network, but who still want to use AI for search, classification or answering questions over their
own documents.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
  <FaqItem question="What hardware do we need?">
    It depends on the model size — smaller models run on a single capable GPU; larger deployments
    we size to the performance you need.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="Can a local model match ChatGPT?">
    Not always on general knowledge, but on a clearly scoped company task — searching documents,
    classification, answering from your own data — yes, and without sending data out.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="Who updates and monitors the model?">
    We set up monitoring and an update plan as part of the project; ongoing support is scoped
    individually to your needs.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="Does it work without an internet connection?">
    Yes, it can run fully offline — that's usually the main reason companies want an on-premise
    deployment.
  </FaqItem>
</Faq>
