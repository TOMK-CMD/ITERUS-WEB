We connect software to ARES, data boxes, the contract registry and the National Electronic Tool
(NEN) — systems most Czech studios don't offer as a named service. Our ARES integration is in
production, and our own internal tool reads public-tender data.

## What can we connect?

We can design and build a connection to ARES (company lookup by registration number), data boxes
(receiving and sending messages), the contract registry and NEN. Fakturoid and the GoPay or Comgate
payment gateways are commercial services, not government systems, but we connect those too.

## What can we show for it?

Our ARES integration runs in production in our own product, Innea, where it verifies companies by
registration number at sign-up. Public-tender data is read by our own internal tool, which
processes TED and ISVZ.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
  <FaqItem question="Can you read data from ISVZ and NEN?">
    Our own internal tool reads public tenders from TED and ISVZ. NEN is a separate registry; we
    design an integration to it based on your requirements.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="Can you handle two-way data-box communication?">
    We can design and connect two-way data-box communication (receiving and sending) to your
    system — the exact solution is built to match what system you already have.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="What if the government changes the API format?">
    Government interfaces change with little warning — we build integrations with tests and
    monitoring that catch a break before it causes damage.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="How do you handle government interface outages?">
    We assume state APIs go down occasionally — we design with a queue and retries so an outage
    doesn't stop the whole process.
  </FaqItem>
</Faq>
