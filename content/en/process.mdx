We work AI-native: delivery is accelerated with Claude Code and Codex, and quality is secured by
automated tests, independent code review and specialists on demand. Who's behind it is on the <PageLink href="/about">About</PageLink> page.

## What does the collaboration look like, step by step?

<ProcessSteps
  name="How Iterus works"
  description="The steps of a project, from the first call to handing over the code."
  href="/process"
>
  <Step name="Introductory call">
    A free 60-minute call — we cover the goal, the scope, and whether we're a good fit.
  </Step>
  <Step name="Discovery (optional)">
    A paid discovery for 25,000 CZK — scope, architecture and estimate. The fee is deducted from
    the price of the follow-up project.
  </Step>
  <Step name="Validation pilot">
    A working version in production: authentication, database, one or two integrations, tests,
    deployment. Takes 3-6 weeks.
  </Step>
  <Step name="Production version">
    Roles and permissions, payments, admin, more integrations, e2e tests, CI and monitoring.
    Takes 6-14 weeks.
  </Step>
  <Step name="Handover">
    The client owns 100% of the source code and documentation on payment — on a standard stack
    another team can pick up.
  </Step>
</ProcessSteps>

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
  <FaqItem question="Who does code review when you work AI-native?">
    Every non-trivial change gets a second pair of eyes regardless of who wrote the code — a human
    reviewer or an independent AI model, and a human always approves the merge. What that looks
    like in practice is in the <PageLink href="/references/innea">Innea</PageLink> case study —
    including why AI review alone is not enough and a human has the final say.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="What if you get sick — who takes over the project?">
    Whoever you choose: the code and documentation are 100% yours on payment, and we build on a
    standard stack (Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL) with automated tests and CI, so another team
    picks the project up without training on a proprietary tool. That this is more than a
    sentence is shown by the <PageLink href="/references">case studies</PageLink> — test and
    migration counts are stated there, not promised.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="Who owns the source code?">
    You do — on payment you own 100% of the source code and documentation — with no dependency on
    us and no proprietary tools that would tie you to us. Handover is part of the price of every band on the
    <PageLink href="/pricing">Pricing</PageLink> page, not an extra.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="Where does our data live?">
    In the EU (Frankfurt), processed under GDPR — the same as our own products' data. If it may not
    leave your company at all, we deploy the
    <PageLink href="/services/local-llm">model locally</PageLink> and the data stays on your
    hardware.
  </FaqItem>
</Faq>
