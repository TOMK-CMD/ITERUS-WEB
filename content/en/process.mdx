We work AI-native: delivery is accelerated with Claude Code and Codex, and quality is secured by
automated tests, independent code review and specialists on demand. Who's behind it is on the <PageLink href="/about">About</PageLink> page.

## What does the collaboration look like, step by step?

<ProcessSteps
  name="How Iterus works"
  description="The steps of a project, from the first call to handing over the code."
  href="/process"
>
  <Step name="Introductory call">
    A free 60-minute call — we cover the goal, the scope, and whether we're a good fit.
  </Step>
  <Step name="Discovery (optional)">
    A paid discovery for 25,000 CZK — scope, architecture and estimate. The fee is deducted from
    the price of the follow-up project.
  </Step>
  <Step name="Validation pilot">
    A working version in production: authentication, database, one or two integrations, tests,
    deployment. Takes 3-6 weeks.
  </Step>
  <Step name="Production version">
    Roles and permissions, payments, admin, more integrations, e2e tests, CI and monitoring.
    Takes 6-14 weeks.
  </Step>
  <Step name="Handover">
    The client owns 100% of the source code and documentation on payment — on a standard stack
    another team can pick up.
  </Step>
</ProcessSteps>

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
  <FaqItem question="Who does code review when you work AI-native?">
    Independent code review is part of the process for every non-trivial change — a second pass
    (human or an independent AI model) looks at the diff regardless of who wrote the code.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="What if you get sick — who takes over the project?">
    The client owns 100% of the code and documentation on payment, and we build on a standard
    stack (Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL) with tests and CI — any team can pick it up without
    training on a proprietary tool.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="Who owns the source code?">
    The client owns 100% of the source code and documentation on payment.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="Where does our data live?">
    Data is hosted in the EU (Frankfurt) and processed under GDPR.
  </FaqItem>
</Faq>
