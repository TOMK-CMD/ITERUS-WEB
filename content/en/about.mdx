Iterus is the software brand of SUN Professionals s.r.o., a company trading since 2005. Software
under the Iterus brand has been built since 2025 — three of the products built since then have
their own <PageLink href="/references">case studies</PageLink> with numbers from the repositories.

## What have we built?

We build production web and AI applications — Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL/Supabase, Claude and
OpenAI API integrations. What that looks like in practice is shown by
<PageLink href="/references/innea">Innea</PageLink> (AI support with a crisis layer),
<PageLink href="/references/innea-pro">Innea Pro</PageLink> (invoicing under Czech rules and
health-insurer billing)
and <PageLink href="/references/legacy-you">Legacy You</PageLink> (a native mobile app for
seniors, pre-launch).

## Who is behind it?

Iterus is run by Tomáš Kučera. Technology has always interested me, and I wanted to build my own
applications for years — running a business never left the time for it. Language models and tools
like Claude Code and Codex changed that: the path from intent to a running application got short
enough to be worth taking. I started with tools for my own company, where I knew the problem best
and carried the cost of any mistake myself. They worked, so I kept going — and Iterus came out of
it.

## Frequently asked questions

<Faq>
  <FaqItem question="How big is the team?">
    The core is one person — the project's technical lead — with AI tools (Claude Code, Codex);
    quality is secured by independent code review. What that means in practice is described in the
    <PageLink href="/references/innea">Innea</PageLink> and
    <PageLink href="/references/legacy-you">Legacy You</PageLink> case studies: who decides, who
    writes the code and who verifies the result against reality. For specialised parts — legal
    text, for instance — we bring in specialists on demand, and we say so up front.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="How long has the company existed?">
    SUN Professionals s.r.o. has traded since 2005; software under the Iterus brand has been built
    since 2025. The company's continuity is on file in the commercial register; judge the software
    experience by the three products with case studies on this site — live or pre-launch, with
    numbers from the repositories — not by the company's age.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="What do you specialise in, and what don't you do?">
    We specialise in web and AI applications and in connections to Czech systems — ARES,
    invoicing, health insurers — the ones Innea Pro is built on. What we don't do — banking core
    systems, penetration testing, medical devices, large enterprise migrations without a dedicated
    team — we say up front. The full list of both is on the
    <PageLink href="/services">Services</PageLink> page.
