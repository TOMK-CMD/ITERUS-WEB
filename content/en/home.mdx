## Why "in loops"?

The name Iterus comes from iteration — a repeated step forward. Good software isn't a leap; it's a
path walked in loops: build, test, listen, improve. Again. With AI tools, iterations are cheaper
and faster than ever — we turn that into a method, and have given it a name.
