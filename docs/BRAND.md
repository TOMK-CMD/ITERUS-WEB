## Name and story
**Iterus** — a coined name inspired by the Latin *iter* (a journey, a path) and by *iterate*.
Say it "ITE-rus" in Czech, "IT-er-us" in English.

Approved copy — CZ:
> Iterus vychází z latinského *iter* — cesta — a ze slova *iterace*. Dobrý software nevzniká jedním
> skokem, ale cestou po krocích: postavit, otestovat, poslouchat, zlepšit. A znovu. Tak pracujeme,
> a tak se jmenujeme.

Approved copy — EN:
> Iterus comes from the Latin *iter* — a journey — and from *iterate*. Good software isn't a leap;
> it's a path walked in loops: build, test, listen, improve. Again.

AI-native tie-in (optional second paragraph): *S AI nástroji jsou iterace levnější a rychlejší
než kdy dřív — my z toho děláme metodu.* / *With AI tools, iterations are cheaper and faster than
ever — we turn that into a method.*

Do not use "Iterum" anywhere (different existing companies). Do not use the tagline
"znovu a lépe" (it is a registered company name of a third party).

## Positioning
AI-native softwarové studio z Prahy. Rychle stavíme produkční webové a AI aplikace — s AI
nástroji a lidským dohledem. / An AI-native software studio from Prague. We build production web
and AI applications fast — with AI tools and human oversight.

Taglines (pick one per surface): CZ *Software, který vzniká po krocích.* · EN *Software, built in
loops.*

## Legal line (mandatory, every page footer, contracts, invoices)
CZ: `Iterus je značka společnosti SUN Professionals s.r.o., IČO 27159884.`
EN: `Iterus is a brand of SUN Professionals s.r.o., Company ID 27159884.`

## Tone of voice
Věcný, sebejistý, důkazy místo superlativů, transparentní o procesu a cenách. Short sentences.
Numbers over adjectives. Czech first, never a translation of English marketing clichés.

## Visual direction (proposal)
- Base: near-black `#0B0D12`, surface `#12151C`, text `#E6E8EE`, muted `#9AA3B2`.
- Accent: electric blue `#3B82F6` (alt: lime `#B6F35B`) — one accent only.
- Type: headings geometric grotesk (Cabinet Grotesk / General Sans), body Inter; mono JetBrains Mono
  for code/metrics.
- Radius 8/12 px, 4-pt spacing scale, motion: 150–250 ms ease-out, staggered reveals, no parallax.
- Imagery: real screenshots and diagrams; no stock photos of handshakes; illustrations generated
  in-house (Higgsfield/Nano Banana) must follow the palette.
- Logo: TODO (wordmark first; symbol optional). Favicon/OG from the same tokens.
